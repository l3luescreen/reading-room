# Reading Room

Your own bookshelf and reading history, kept on the SD card.
No account and no internet.

How it works:
- Each time you finish reading (leave a book or put the reader to sleep),
  the reader notes the book, when you read, for how long, and how far you
  got. This needs CrossPoint 2.0 or later, and the clock must have been set
  once (any Wi-Fi connection sets it).
- Open the reader's web page (File Transfer) and go to Plugins. Reading Room
  collects the new sessions into "/Reading Room/" on the SD card and shows
  your shelf and statistics.

Important: the reader keeps only about 14 sessions waiting. Open the
Plugins page at least that often (for example once a week), or older
waiting sessions are lost.

After installing, restart the reader once.
